Example — Dental practice (Healthcare, United Kingdom, 4 weeks, illustrative)
  Challenge: one receptionist covering phone, front desk and billing; calls
      during treatments went to voicemail; evening enquiries answered next
      morning; no reminders; WhatsApp on the principal's personal phone.
  Before: Patient calls → voicemail → callback next morning · Website form →
      inbox → replied when quiet · No reminders → daily no-shows · WhatsApp on a
      personal phone, nothing logged.
  Built: AI website answers hours, prices and prep questions instantly ·
      Qualification collects treatment and urgency, then offers real slots ·
      Booking lands on the right dentist's calendar · Reminders at 24 h and 2 h
      with a reschedule link · Missed call → automatic text with the booking
      link · Review request 2 hours after each visit.
  Results (targets): 35% faster lead response · 12 hrs per week of admin
      removed · 20–30% fewer no-shows.
  Takeaway: Reception was never the problem. The gap between a patient's
      interest and a human reply was.

TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
- Static site, no build. Config in site.config.js; catalog content in data.js.
- Booking: Calendly (calendly.url). Cal.com supported as fallback.
- Chat: chat.js → /api/chat (Vercel function, Groq key server-side) with
  rule-based fallback. dev-server.py proxies locally.
- Hooks: contact form fields → any endpoint (Web3Forms/Formspree/n8n webhook);
  Calendly event_scheduled message → success state (and a webhook on the
  Calendly side for reminder sequences and post-call surveys).
- Deployed: GitHub Pages (static) at https://saikiranreddy18.github.io/ds_agency/
  Vercel import pending for the chat backend.
- Deliberately not built: animated counters with invented totals, client
  logo wall, "recent wins" ticker, country filter.
