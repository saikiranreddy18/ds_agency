What if our tools aren't supported?

Most tools have a way to connect (an API), an export, or an email trigger we can work with. If one really can't connect, we'll say so and suggest a simple swap or a manual step. We'll check your stack in the strategy call.

Do we need to change how we work?

No. We build around the steps you already follow and only remove the ones that don't need a person. Your team keeps the tools it knows, with less copy-paste. Tell us how a normal day runs and we'll map what stays and what goes.

What if automation isn't worth it yet?

Sometimes it isn't. If you get a handful of enquiries a week, a simple checklist may be all you need for now. If it's not worth it, we'll tell you honestly.

Who actually builds this?

The engineer you speak to on the strategy call. There is no sales team and no hand-off, so the person who understands your workflow is the one who builds it. Book a call and you'll meet them on day one.
