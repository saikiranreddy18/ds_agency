Why we exist

The founder spent years building AI agents, real-time systems and integrations for software products. Every small business owner they met told the same story: a phone that could not be answered mid-job, quotes written at midnight, customers lost to whoever replied first. The technology to fix this had become cheap and dependable. Nobody was assembling it for businesses this size.

So that is the job. Take automations proven at scale, rebuild them on affordable tools, and put them in the hands of the businesses that gain the most from them.

The problem we solve

Small businesses lose customers and hours in the gaps between their tools: between a website form and a reply, between a booking and a reminder, between a finished job and an invoice. Each gap waits for a person. We close the gaps so people only step in where they add value.

How we choose technology

  • Boring where possible. Proven tools with free or low-cost plans. Novelty only where it earns its place.
  • Your accounts, always. Every automation runs in accounts registered to you. If we disappeared tomorrow, nothing would stop.
  • AI with limits. Assistants answer only from your material, say when they do not know, and hand off to a human with context.
  • Everything logged. Every run of every automation is recorded and alerts on failure.
