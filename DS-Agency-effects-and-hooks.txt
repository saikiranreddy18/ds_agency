1. PLUG-IN POINTS FOR AUTOMATION (search for "PLUG IN" in the code)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

every form falls back to a mailto: link to SITE.email with the fields in the
body. Set it to a Web3Forms / Formspree URL, or an n8n / Make webhook, and
every form below POSTs multipart FormData with these fields:
